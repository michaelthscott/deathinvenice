today nothing

planned on walking in the woods early - all day fresh air revisiting mid summer paths

but as i sat at the bus stop i remembered id meant to take the disposable camera to use up the last pic in front of the lake

having a theory of obeying this kind of realization rather than dismiss it - i went back to get the camera - and the phone rang - it was a bi guy from gayromeo that id messaged out of idle curiosity a couple of days before - he wanted me to fuck him - i really didnt think he was my type but he seemed pleasant and in web work - swap fuck for work info i reckoned - took the chance of catching his call as destiny and said yes

off i went down the kudamm and bought norwegian wood and the lonely planet for singapore and malaysia

he was an hour late so i read the history of malaysia - all day should have been like that

he was gentle friendly - maybe somewhere nervous but i couldnt see it - i was neither attracted or repelled - casually in a different dimension id volunteered to fuck him - we looked at each other momentarily with such a distant look - what? - ive seen it before in these situations - like both of us are wondering if we should back out but dont - sometimes someone is in your story - sometimes you are in theirs - sometimes both - sometimes almost neither

how uninterested i was in his body

but he took it well and i performed with some narrowly focused pleasure - it was the straight boys thing of wanting to be the girl for a change - once i was done it was done

we shivered while he smoked on the balcony - this was the balcony of summer - the shriveled leaves in front of us - the cold - the moment to feel that summer had gone

on the sofa i felt a million miles down in my thoughts - coming up briefly in waves - struggling to actually feel like we were talking - monologue - he didnt seem to notice and was quite cheerful in a quiet way

hes from sydney - his girlfriends german - he made a point of saying that he chose to be here - but our conversation said that there was no reason to live in berlin unless one was tied here

my stomach was rumbling so i resolved to go to the japanese and eat - yesterday before the movie i had only a starbucks cheese sandwich and carrot cake

on going out the door i knew there was some gesture missing - i dont think he was expecting anything - but maybe i am cold - too cold and distant - the older man

on the stairs there were two suspicious punks - a boy and girl - early 20s - they looked like they shouldnt have been there - i stared hard at them and they stared back - in london i would probably have asked them why they were there

a short distance up the street the punk boy came after us and did a speech in german - something to do with a doctor and a sister - he showed me a photo and asked me if it was me - was i the guy? the doctor? - i had to put my glasses on to look at the photo - it obviously wasnt me - but the punk wasnt so sure - i felt certain they were going to be on the stairs when i got back - that fate had me in its jaws - stabbed for some germans sin - but no - theyd gone

somehow i did nothing today

except buy two books and hand in the camera to get developed
