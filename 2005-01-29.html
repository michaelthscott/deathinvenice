Happy Home [2005-01-29 00:47:]
I went on a bit of a spending spree on Abebooks the other day.

One of the things I bought was a copy of What is Remembered by Alice B. Toklas.

Imagine my delight on discovering that I had rescued a lovable library book from a heartless grave.



For almost 23 years – from '67 to '92 – it sat on a Kentucky library shelf without so much as a single check out.



And then a further 12 years of second-hand limbo before technology enabled me to rescue her.
