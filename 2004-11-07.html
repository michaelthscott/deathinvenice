Once upon a time, I, a handkerchief dropped on the pavement, dreamt I was a butterfly, fluttering hither and thither, to all intents and purposes a butterfly.

I was conscious only of my happiness as a butterfly, unaware that I was a discarded handkerchief.

Soon I awaked, and there I was, veritably myself again.

Now I do not know whether I was then a handkerchief dreaming I was a butterfly, or whether I am now a butterfly, dreaming I am a handkerchief.
