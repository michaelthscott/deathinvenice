Back to the madhouse [2004-04-03 17:00]
[mood need a snooze]
When I told Dai that we were returning to London, he said "Back to reality"; but actually, hopping back and forth between London and Berlin, the impression gained is that London is mad, divorced from reality; to arrive there is to feel one has sunk into a runaway theatrical experiment in overcrowding and pretentiousness.

By comparison, Berlin, feels comfortably non-event full, like "reality" is assumed to be.

These, of course, are just relative impressions, and will be swept up and forgotten in the next couple of months.

So, on to the new home, two floors above a (currently empty) shop with a tiny bathroom in the sky. Maybe it will be silent. At least it is in the right location. Sanity in London depends on evening access to Hyde Park.

The rent is £375 per week.
