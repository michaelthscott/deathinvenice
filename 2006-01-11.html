ghosts [2006-01-11 22:46]

woke at 3 in the morning - from a dream of shirt sizes and an impending explosion - to the sound of the blind fluttering - not in a long time had an empty house seemed so spooky - i could feel the draught from the half-opened window like a breath - cold on my shoulder - ebbing and flowing - the rattling blind sounded like a restless voice - in a foreign language - rasping and stuttering - yes - that was it - it was almost flamenco

the week before - i'd gone to virgin records in piccadilly - it's my habit when on my own for a while - to get a soundtrack for solitude - as i entered my usual hunting ground - the world folk and jazz section - immediately - out of the corner of my eye - i caught a bland round face looking out - waiting - but i walk on by - rummaging for a good 20 minutes - weighing up potential purchases and rejecting them - nothing compelled - until - unintentionally - there i was back in front of the bland round face - looking out - waiting - so addicted did i become that i went back three times in the week for more grabaciones discos pizarra - so far i have six of them - ah obsessions - bless 'em
