mystic [2006-08-06 12:08]

the village is perched on an abyss - a grassy path threads its way out to a bench - right on the very edge of the emptiness - wisps of dragon cloud unfurl below you - swallows cruise the air with ease - it's hard to look down

it's hard not to look down

every evening at twilight a tall stern looking man - the philosopher - draped in a serious blanket - departs his cheerful home - and with steady tread establishes himself above the void - eyes closed in meditation

i remember i used to think that the mind could transcend itself - with investigation and effort - some kind of mystical transformation would be possible - Don Juan - Castaneda - drug altered reality - but now that does seem just the stuff of words - the birdlike freedom of literature - in all its forms and guises

the mind is just a practical tool - which with luck remains practical for long - but on its edges - in the slipstream of daily adventures - literature seeds its mystical moments - moments when we know maybe what nothing is - the true nothing - the nothing that is never not anything
