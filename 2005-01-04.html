Devil's Asshole [2005-01-04 23:36]
I usually say the experience of getting between the start of Christmas and the end of the New Year is like crawling through the Devil's asshole.



It wasn't so bad this year. For three days of holiday I got ten days off work.

Guangtao came to stay and we became amateur cameramen roaming the cold but sunny tourist zone.



I went to places I hadn't been for years and looked for the photographs that no one takes.

In fact these two took themselves...



While Guangtao was busy framing a couple of kids feeding seagulls I set my zoom to max and pointed at the busy sky – it's uncropped I swear.



We were at Camden Market on the bridge trying to photograph the moon. For some reason my flash had got set to on, no red eye – it's usually off. It was only when I got home that found I'd startled Tadzio.
