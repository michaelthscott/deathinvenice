smell of turps [2005-08-03 01:03]

it was late - i'd been proving my manhood - so i knocked together a quick stir fry - little shards of carrot - chunks of mushroom - courgette - fish balls with sea moss - cut in half - and some scallop noodles thrown in - with a dash of sesame oil - a man needs sustaining - a small can of cold beer - first in months - i'm tired of worrying about what i eat - doesn't seem to make any difference anyway - i sat on the muji armchair and ate - in the dark room - by the light of the anglepoise beside me - the charmed circle open on my knees - time passed - it passed - i ate - i read - i slurped and scraped - and set my empty bowl beside me - i was a little tipsy - soft headed and amused - i read - and then i noticed - inside my greasy bowl - with its chopstick scrapings - was a painting - just like the ones i used to make

authenticity - naturalness - non professional - these words have elected themselves guardians of the i want to be an artist daydream that still remains in idle moments - i wish i knew exactly how to live up to their expectations
