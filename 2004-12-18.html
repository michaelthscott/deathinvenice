Domestic Violence [2004-12-18 23:04]
The trouble with living in a tree house, or up in the crow's nest, is that you've got to go up and down those bloody stairs, day after day, morning after early morning, until you sort of forget where you are, what you are doing, and then you slip.



The last time I did it it wasn't so bad. Just a wooosh and bump bump bump. But the other morning, sleepy headed and in the dark, as I went down I got my foot caught behind me. Man that was bad. I thought I was done for. Sixty five kilos fall like a rock. You know then just how heavy you are. A startled lump going thump.

Anyway, not to worry. I limped a bit to a chair and got my breath back. Walked gingerly for a while. Applied gallons of arnica cream and whistled like nothing was wrong. That was a couple of days ago. I survived.

So much for groovy felt slippers. Maybe I need a pair of these.
