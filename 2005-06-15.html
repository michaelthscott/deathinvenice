zabriskie point [2005-06-15 01:09]

i've been thinking - not very coherently - about eras - recently - not knowing really how we move from one to another - but there is a feeling of starting a new era

gertrude stein has been telling me at lunchtime how some men are good at starting - how they have an important feeling in them that comes from being good at starting - this important feeling inside them makes them feel as big as the whole world around them

being good at starting doesn't necessarily make one good at continuing - in some men this being bad at continuing leads to a strong constant impatient feeling in them - they dwindle - become old ones - become dead ones

there are others - she says - and i want to be one - who can always be starting a new beginning

i left dai - coffee ended - and drifted down towards the south bank - it was too early to go home but it was cold - i didn't really know why i was doing what i was doing - the back of my mind debating whether to go to the sauna at waterloo - being practical i knew i might as well go half way undecided and renew my membership at the nft

as i was crossing the hungerford bridge - i reflected - quite consciously - that it was as if i was allowing myself to be pulled by an unknown force of events - one which i would understand eventually - as i strolled in to the nft i was a bit dumbfounded by the timing - zabriskie point would start in two minutes

long ago - in a previous era - i had rushed out elated after seeing those slow motion exploding fridges and book shelves - rushed out into the champs elysee paris summer evening pavement - running down - along - with long bounding steps out into the somewhere - nowhere - tear it all down - era of elated youth
