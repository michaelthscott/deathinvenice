View past my coffee cup (Café Nero) [2004-07-17 22:06]
[music Eighteen Tragic Parts Of Barbarian Pipes]

I woke early one morning a few weeks ago and looked out the bedroom window down onto the jumble of backs and roofs and watched a ginger cat creeping cautiously along the top of the nursery school wall. Something was odd, and at that moment a fox face turned and looked up at me. Suddenly the "cat" was a cunning "dog" that had somehow outreached itself and was wondering how to get down. It wobbled on like a man in heels and was gone behind a wall.

These kitsune live in the kitchen waiting inspiration and a nail.



Meanwhile today I penetrated the crowds as far as Covent Garden and idled on the way back. This was supposed to be a view of the cafe where I took the previous shot, but it's not - it's behind the cup.
