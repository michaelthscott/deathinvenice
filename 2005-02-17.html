Apparently the architect insisted that all the fire extinguishers in the museum should be painted white. They seemed sadly anemic, denied of their colour.

I had a long talk with Mario about not making art. The first of several sketches over the weekend of the following theory:

The DVD Poet

Poetry, in contrast to the other arts, is basically non-commercial. No one expects to become a full time professional poet. It's ok for poets to have other jobs. The few exceptions prove the rule.

The important thing about being non-commercial is that it allows production to be occasional. One need only produce as the spirit moves. Quality can remain in its rightful place, superior to quantity.

Written (as opposed to spoken) poetry is really just page layout, book design.

Traditionally poets have concerned themselves with little more than line breaks and indentation, perhaps a font change or two. Once again a few exceptions proves the rule.

Today poets can do their own book design. Where once they stopped at the manuscript, now they can continue. One day it will be hard to tell where poetry stops and book design begins.

With book design comes illustration, the image. Cheap, lightweight digital cameras enable poets to accumulate word and image, even sound. All material to compose into a "poem".

Substitute DVD for book. No need to contract to a publisher. Copies are cheap.

In the not to distant future DVD poets are burning discs to give away.

Plenty of familiar faces. Small talk, big talk. Standing up, sitting down. Then off in taxies to the Café Olympíco where the two João's had organized a little party in the billiard hall.
