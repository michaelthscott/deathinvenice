the healing game [2006-02-19 23:41]
i was back in belfast a couple of weeks ago - one of the things i did was to go see van morrison sing - after the concert i was standing in the car park - waiting to tell my niece - now you know you come from the land of the voice - when my brother started to drive over my foot - he was reversing - i was in his blind spot - cars are big and heavy when they steadily roll up the edge of your shoe - the tyre dug into my ankle

i didn't think about it much until a couple of days later - it hurt - it was red and swollen - infected - oh yes - i thought - infection - i'd forgotten about that - so what followed was disinfecting - ointments and bandaging - in a few days it was better - all that's now left is a little crust of hard skin

last weekend - on my own again - i was crossing the park - off to the french institute - to see alexei german's khrustalyov, my car! - it was damp - slowly raining - big juicy drops sparkling along the hawthorn branches - some small leaves coming out

all these complex processes - inside and out - and sandwiched between them the simplicity of being a person - any person - this person - people - its strange how little is known - how much is ignored - my neck is stiff - i need a new pair of shoes - can't find a pair that i like
